The standard

What research use only
means here.

Every material we supply is a laboratory reagent. This statement is on the label, the insert, the listing and the invoice, and it is the basis on which we sell.

Not a drug or a supplement

Not drugs, biologics, supplements, food, cosmetics or devices. Not FDA-approved or authorised for any use.

No claims, ever

We publish no dosing, administration or protocol information, and claim no safety or efficacy for any condition.

Business buyers only

Sale is restricted to verified businesses and institutions that sign the research-use terms.

Printed on every vial alongside compound, strength, lot, storage condition and the supplying entity.

Zero-touch CoA

How a certificate reaches your customer.

01

Batch receipt

Each lot is registered against a unique batch identifier at the node, with chain of custody and storage conditions logged from the first hour.

02

Independent assay

Samples go to accredited third-party US laboratories. Purity, identity and water content return against the batch identifier.

03

Document binding

The signed certificate is ingested, validated and bound to the batch record. No manual uploads and no version drift.

04

Customer linkage

Every vial carries a scannable reference that resolves the exact current certificate, including after a re-test.

Certificates report analytical properties only. They are not a statement of safety, efficacy or suitability for any use in humans or animals.

Operating standard

Documented by default.

R-01

Third-party testing

Every batch assayed by accredited US labs. No result from an in-house bench, no certificate without a live lab reference.

R-02

Chain of custody

Each vial bound to its batch identifier from receipt to dispatch, with temperature and handling events logged against it.

R-03

Seven-year retention

Certificates, shipping records and custody logs stay retrievable for the life of your account, including after a re-test.

R-04

Claims review

Partner email, product and marketing copy is reviewed before it publishes. Therapeutic, dosing and human-use language is rejected.

Accountability

One group, three functions, one standard.

We are the supplier, the software company and the fulfillment operator. Owning all three means responsibility for what leaves our nodes sits with us, so the conditions below are enforced rather than merely published.

Supply

Sourcing and release

Incoming QC, independent lab testing and batch release. No lot enters a node without a registered identifier and a signed certificate.

Software

Storefront and claims

Because we build the storefronts, we review the copy on them. Therapeutic, dosing and human-use language is blocked before it publishes.

Fulfillment

Storage and dispatch

Cold-chain handling and shipping from four live US nodes, with chain of custody logged against the batch from receipt to carrier.

A partner who breaks the research-use terms is not only a problem for their own business. Because the same group supplied the material, built the store and shipped the parcel, it becomes a problem for ours. That is why verification is a real check, why claims review is mandatory, and why the restrictions below are enforced at the point of fulfillment rather than left to good intentions.

Cross-border supply

US live today. Canada is next, not yet open.

We would rather run the US network properly than launch two countries at once. The Canadian nodes are in preparation, and the standard below is written to extend to them as they open.

Domestic dispatch, US

Orders ship from our four US nodes to a US business address. Your customer receives a domestic parcel with domestic tracking, and no customs entry is made in their name.

Canada — not yet open for turnkey fulfillment

Ontario and British Columbia nodes are in preparation. Canadian businesses can contact the partnerships desk directly and we will bring you on as the network opens.

Materials are not authorised drugs

Nothing in the catalogue holds a US drug approval, a Health Canada DIN or NPN, or any marketing authorisation in either country. They are laboratory reagents and are described as such everywhere they appear.

Your market, your obligations

You are responsible for meeting the requirements of every market you sell into, including any import, licensing or record-keeping duty that applies to your own customers.

Requirements differ between the United States and Canada and change over time. Canadian regulatory advice is a separate exercise from US advice and will be taken before that network opens, and we can introduce counsel who work in this category.

Conditions of supply

Who we will not supply, and what ends an account.

Written into the supply agreement and enforced at verification, at claims review and at fulfillment.

We will not supply

  • Consumers, patients or the general public
  • Clinics, prescribers or anyone sourcing for administration to people or animals
  • Compounding pharmacies and outsourcing facilities
  • Anyone declining verification or the research-use terms
  • Jurisdictions where supply of the material is restricted

What ends an account

  • Publishing dosing, protocol or human-use claims
  • Removing the research-use statement from labels or listings
  • Marketing to consumers or patients on any channel
  • Reselling into a compounding or clinical supply chain
  • Bypassing the order-level attestation
