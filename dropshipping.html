Peptide 3PL

What dedicated peptide fulfillment actually covers.

A general 3PL stores ambient pallets. Peptide fulfillment runs at 2–8°C, rotates by expiry, binds documentation to every unit, and labels the parcel under your brand.

Temperature-controlled storage

Lyophilized vials held at 2–8°C across all four live US nodes. Handling events are logged against the batch identifier from the moment a lot arrives.

FEFO rotation

The earliest-expiring batch ships first, automatically. Nothing ages out on a shelf while newer stock moves ahead of it — no batch sits past its window because a picker took the wrong unit.

Lot-accurate documentation

Every shipped vial carries the batch identifier that links it to its certificate. Purity, identity and water content from an accredited US lab, verified at the time of the assay, not copied from a previous batch.

White-label dispatch

Plain outer packaging under your label. Your customer receives a domestic parcel from your brand, with a batch CoA card enclosed and tracking in your storefront. Our name does not appear on the shipment.

Cold-chain packaging

Insulated mailers and cold-chain appropriate packaging selected per shipment, matched to transit time and nearest node.

Compliance at dispatch

Each order carries the research-use attestation captured at your checkout. Orders without one on file are held, not shipped.

Seven-year records

Shipping records, batch documents and custody logs stay retrievable for seven years, including after a re-test on the same lot.

Peptide dropshipping FAQ

Questions operators ask before signing.

What is peptide dropshipping?
Peptide dropshipping means the supplier holds and ships inventory on your behalf, under your brand, when a customer places an order on your storefront. You market and sell; the supplier stores, labels and dispatches each unit. In the EVLV model, we supply the inventory as well as fulfilling it — your customer receives a domestic parcel from your brand, with a batch CoA card enclosed, and our name does not appear anywhere on the shipment.
How is peptide dropshipping different from a standard 3PL?
A standard 3PL takes your own inventory and ships it for you. In the EVLV model, we supply the inventory as well as fulfilling it — which means no capital is tied up in stock on your side beyond the opening order, and no minimum reorder beyond your own selling pace. We source, test, hold and dispatch; you market and take orders.
Do I need cold storage or a warehouse?
No. EVLV holds all inventory at 2–8°C across four US cold-chain nodes. You never touch the product. Your opening order is real inventory held for you at your assigned node and drawn down as orders ship.
What does each order look like to my customer?
A domestic parcel under your brand label, with a batch-specific certificate of analysis card enclosed. Tracking is written back to your storefront as the parcel moves. Our name does not appear anywhere on the shipment or the paperwork.
Who can my customers be?
Verified business and institutional research buyers only. Each order captures a research-use attestation at checkout. EVLV does not fulfil to consumers, clinics, compounding pharmacies or anyone who cannot demonstrate a legitimate research use. This is enforced at dispatch, not just at signup.
Is peptide dropshipping available in Canada?
US fulfillment is live today across four nodes. Canadian fulfillment is in preparation. Canadian businesses can apply and note it, or email the partnerships desk directly — we will reach out as the Canadian nodes open.
