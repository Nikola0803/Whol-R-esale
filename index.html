Questions

Before you apply.

What does it cost to start?
A $5,000 opening stock order at your wholesale tier — real inventory held at your node and drawn down as you sell. There is no build fee and no software fee on top of it. We make our margin on the product, which is why the opening order is stock and not an access fee.
Do I need to hold inventory after that?
No. We hold stock in our US cold-chain nodes and ship each order under your label. You reorder as you sell — no warehouse, no lease, and no ongoing capital beyond your own reordering pace.
How is my wholesale pricing set?
By four inputs: committed monthly volume, product mix across the catalogue, fulfillment model (bulk cases to your facility or turnkey shipping from our nodes), and verification status. There is no public price list. Your tier is confirmed in writing during onboarding and reviewed quarterly against what you actually ship.
Can I keep the site I already run?
Yes. If you run WordPress, a plugin syncs your catalogue, tier pricing and live stock, and pushes paid orders back to us for fulfillment. For everything else there is a REST API over the same layer, with sandbox keys issued alongside your partner key. Our storefront is an option, not a condition.
Is the shipping really blind?
Yes, by default. Your label is on the vial, your brand is on the carton and the return address is configured to your account. Nothing in the parcel names EVLV, and tracking is written back into your own storefront.
Who can buy from a partner storefront?
Verified business and institutional research buyers. Every order captures a research-use attestation at checkout. We do not fulfil to consumers, clinics or compounding pharmacies, and that restriction passes through to your storefront as a condition of the account.
What documentation ships with an order?
A batch-specific certificate of analysis from an independent accredited US laboratory, linked by QR on the vial label and enclosed as a card. Records are retained for seven years, including after a re-test on the same lot.

Business verification, then we build.

A short application, a decision in two to three business days, and a $5,000 opening stock order once you clear it. That order is real inventory at your wholesale tier, held at your node — not an access fee.
