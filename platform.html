Integrations

Already have a site?
Keep it. We will plug into it.

You do not have to move to our storefront to become a partner. If you already run WordPress, or you have your own stack and a developer, connect it to our catalogue and sell from the site you have.

Route 1

Our storefront

We build it from your brand files during onboarding, on your domain, with the CRM wired in. Nothing to install and nothing to maintain.

Route 2

WordPress plugin

One plugin on your existing WooCommerce site. Your catalogue, wholesale pricing and stock levels sync from us, and orders sync back for fulfillment.

Route 3

REST API

Build against the same endpoints our own storefronts use. Products, live stock by node, order creation, tracking and certificates.

WordPress

What the plugin keeps in sync.

Install it, paste your partner key, choose which products to list. From then on the two catalogues stay matched without anyone touching a spreadsheet.

Pulled from us

  • Products, specifications, CAS and lot data
  • Your wholesale tier, with your markup rules applied to generate retail prices
  • Live stock by node, so a sold-out compound stops selling on your site too
  • Batch certificate links, refreshed automatically after a re-test
  • The research-use statement and checkout attestation block

Pushed back to us

  • Paid orders, routed straight to the nearest node
  • The buyer attestation captured at your checkout
  • Address and shipping selection for domestic routing
  • Cancellations and address corrections before dispatch

Tracking numbers and status changes come back to your order records as the parcel moves.

The plugin is supplied to verified partners and updates itself. Claims review still applies to the copy on your own site — the compliance blocks it installs are the minimum, not a substitute for the review.

API

Or skip the plugin
and build it yourself.

A REST API over the same catalogue, stock and fulfillment layer. Keyed per partner, scoped to your account, rate-limited and documented.

  • Read products, specifications and your tier pricing
  • Read live inventory per node before you promise a delivery date
  • Create orders with the buyer attestation attached
  • Read dispatch status, tracking and batch certificates
  • Subscribe to webhooks instead of polling

Sandbox credentials are issued with your partner key so you can build before your first real order.
