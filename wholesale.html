Choosing a supplier

What to look for in a peptide wholesale supplier.

Not all wholesale relationships are equal. These are the four questions worth asking before you commit to any supplier.

Who holds the inventory?

Some programmes are resellers with a markup on top of a third-party supplier. EVLV sources, tests, holds and ships from its own cold-chain nodes. There is no middleman in the fulfillment chain, which means one chain of custody and one place the documentation sits.

What does the testing look like?

Ask for the laboratory name, its accreditation number and the test date. A legitimate certificate of analysis names an independent accredited laboratory. A certificate with no lab reference, or a result from an in-house bench, is not a document you can hand to a buyer or an auditor.

Is there a real minimum?

A wholesale supplier with no minimum order is selling at a slight discount, not running a wholesale programme. The $5,000 minimum here is real inventory at real wholesale prices, drawn down against your orders — not an access fee or a platform subscription.

Can you keep your own storefront?

Your domain, your customer relationships and your brand equity are yours. A good wholesale supplier plugs into your existing site or builds one on your domain — and never requires you to send your customers somewhere else to buy.

Peptide wholesale FAQ

What to know before you sign a wholesale agreement.

What is the minimum peptide wholesale order?
The minimum opening stock order is currently $5,000 at your wholesale tier. This is real inventory purchased at wholesale prices, held for you at your assigned US cold-chain node, and drawn down as your first orders ship. It is not an access fee or a deposit — you own the product from the moment it is allocated to your account.
How does peptide wholesale pricing work?
Wholesale pricing is tiered by four inputs: committed monthly volume, product mix across the catalogue, fulfillment model (bulk cases to your facility, or turnkey shipping from our nodes under your label), and verification status. There is no public price list. Your tier is confirmed in writing during onboarding and reviewed quarterly against what you actually ship.
Do I need to hold my own inventory after the opening order?
No. After the opening order activates your account, we hold stock in our US cold-chain nodes on your behalf. You reorder as you sell. There is no ongoing warehouse, no recurring capital beyond your reorder pace, and no product sitting at your facility.
What does partner verification involve?
Entity verification (incorporation documents and officer identification), signed research-use terms, and a review of your intended market and marketing approach. Verification typically takes two to three business days from a complete application. No pricing is released and no build begins before it clears.
Can I keep my own domain and website?
Yes, and you do not have to move to our storefront. If you run WordPress, a WooCommerce plugin syncs our catalogue, your tier pricing and live stock, and pushes paid orders back to us for fulfillment. If you run a custom stack, a REST API covers the same catalogue, stock and order layer, with sandbox keys issued alongside your partner credentials.
What documents ship with a wholesale order?
A batch-specific certificate of analysis from an independent accredited US laboratory, enclosed with every shipment and linked by QR code on the vial label. Records are retained for seven years, including after a re-test on the same lot.
